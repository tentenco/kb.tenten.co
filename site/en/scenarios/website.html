New website or brand redesign

From positioning, content and platform choice to a production handoff.

Read the first article →See all steps ↓
What you will deliver

A clear brief, site architecture and prototype, plus a buildable, accepted website and handoff.

01Set the direction02Build the experience03Verify and launch
01

Set the direction

Define whose problem the project solves.

  1. Project kickoff and digital consulting →

    Put the problem, constraints and decisions on one page before building.

    2 min

  2. Brand positioning and messaging →

    Decide who you serve, what you solve and why someone should choose you.

    2 min
02

Build the experience

Choose the journey, platform and delivery standards.

  1. UX, information architecture and conversion →

    Help people find information, understand choices and finish important tasks.

    2 min

  2. Choosing Webflow, Shopify, WordPress or headless →

    Choose for content, transactions and operations, including long-term cost.

    2 min

  3. Development delivery and quality →

    Deliver working software that others can test and maintain.

    2 min
03

Verify and launch

Check measurement and the production environment.

  1. Launch measurement: GA4, GSC, Google Ads and Meta →

    Decide what to measure, then connect traffic, behavior and transaction data.

    2 min

  2. Website launch: readiness and cutover →

    Check SEO, transactions, tracking, DNS and recovery before launch.

    2 min

  • Audience, business goals and brand assets
