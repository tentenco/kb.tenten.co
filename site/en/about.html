About this library

Short guides. Shared experience.

Find an article for your task, understand the concept and reason, then follow the steps.

How to use it

  • Find guidance through a work guide, topic or search.
  • Each article covers the concept, impact, main points, example, actions and checks.
  • Open the handoff section at the end, add project context, and copy or download the Markdown.
  • Agents work within existing permissions and deliver results with evidence. People make tradeoffs and accept the work.

Scope of this edition

  • This edition contains 32 suggested guides based on Tenten pages, official documentation, the XTERRA snapshot and general practices. Erik’s complete private notes and cases from the past 20 years were not supplied.
  • XTERRA code and documents were reviewed read-only. Production accounts, sending flows and complete migration data were not verified. Repository descriptions do not establish production acceptance.
  • FDE embeds engineering delivery in the client workflow. Scope, acceptance and operating responsibility depend on the project agreement.
  • Drafts, expanded sections and reading position stay in this tab, with clear and undo controls. Theme preference stays in this browser. No data is sent to external services. Do not enter passwords or tokens.

Keep the knowledge useful

  • Add project lessons to the relevant article before creating another document.
  • Record the situation, reason, approach, evidence and limits. Keep client details in the authorized project workspace.
  • Recheck sources and dates after platform changes, failures or evidence that changes the advice.
  • The team still needs to agree on maintainers and a review schedule.
