Google & YouTube
Install the Google & YouTube sales channel from the Shopify App Store.
The Google & YouTube channel automatically syncs your products and relevant information about your Shopify store with the
Google Merchant Center
. You can then update your Google product listings directly from your Shopify admin. For eligible stores selling in the United States, products synced to Google Merchant Center using the Google & YouTube channel can display in relevant
Google Shopping tab search listings
for free.

Google & YouTube channel requirements

considerations before syncing an existing Google Merchant Center account
