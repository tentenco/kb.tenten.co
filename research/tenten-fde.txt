METHODOLOGY · FDE
One workflow, from scope to operations
The same team understands the work, builds the integration and plans the operating responsibility. A clear first scope lets you review working software and make the next decision with evidence.
Four delivery principles
01
Understand the actual workflow
Work with the people doing the task. Confirm data, permissions, exceptions and the decisions the system must support.
02
Agree on the first scope
Choose one use case with an owner, a baseline and acceptance criteria. Compare benefit, integration cost and risk before the build.
03
Review working progress
Use working iterations and documented decisions to keep progress visible and adjust the scope when evidence changes.
04
Define the operating owner
Evaluation, monitoring, incident response and knowledge transfer belong in the scope before launch.
Evaluate, build, then operate
PHASE 1 · EVALUATE
Define the workflow
Map users, sources and constraints. Agree on success criteria and decide whether the next stage is justified.
PHASE 2 · BUILD
Build and review together
Connect the agreed systems, review working iterations and test the main path and exceptions with users.
PHASE 3 · OPERATE
Launch with responsibility
Confirm release conditions, monitoring and support. Expand after the first workflow meets its acceptance criteria.
