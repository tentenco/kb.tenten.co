Online Store
Your Shopify online store is an online home for your business. You can use it to create webpages, publish a blog, and sell your products.
You need to be on the Basic plan or higher to sell with an online store. If you're new to Shopify, then
learn how to get started
.
On the
Agentic plan
, you can build an online store, but you need to move to a paid Shopify subscription plan to publish it. Learn more about
Online store access with the Agentic plan
.
Your online store is flexible, so you can build your business with the following customizations:
build custom menus and store filters to help customers navigate your store
add a custom domain
set up accelerated checkout buttons for your product pages
design your online store using responsive themes
To get started quickly, follow the
online store setup guide
.
